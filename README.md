# Supermarket-Sales-Performance-Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project aims to analyze supermarket sales performance using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, understand customer behavior, and evaluate product performance.

The dashboard helps users explore sales data from different perspectives and gain insights that can support data-driven business decisions.


## Project Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze monthly sales trends and average order value.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Understand customer profiles, membership status, and customer segments.
- Identify top-performing product categories by sales revenue and profit.
- Provide interactive filters for exploring different aspects of business performance.

## Business Questions

This dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

- How does sales revenue change over time?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which customer segments generate the most sales?
- How does average order value change over time?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue and profit?
- How does customer membership status relate to sales performance?

## Key Findings & Business Insights


### 1. Customer Demographics
- Married customers account for approximately **50% of the total customer base**, making them the largest marital-status group.
- This suggests an opportunity to better understand the shopping needs and purchasing behavior of married customers.

### 2. Product Performance
- Grocery, meat, and fruits are among the leading product categories in sales revenue and quantity sold.
- These categories represent important product groups to monitor when planning inventory, promotions, and product availability.

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis
- Regular shoppers, family shoppers, and budget shoppers are the major customer segments in the analysis.
- These segments contribute significantly to customer count and sales revenue, highlighting the importance of understanding their shopping preferences and spending patterns.

### 4. Sales Performance Trends
- The monthly revenue and average order value charts suggest potential declines in sales performance compared with the previous year.
- This may indicate changes in customer spending or purchasing frequency and warrants further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Customer targeting:** Analyze purchasing patterns among married customers and family shoppers to identify relevant product bundles and promotions.
- **Product strategy:** Maintain the availability of key categories such as groceries, meat, and fruits while evaluating their profitability.
- **Customer engagement:** Develop targeted promotions for regular shoppers and family shoppers to encourage repeat purchases.
- **Sales improvement:** Investigate changes in average order value and monthly revenue to determine whether the decline is related to lower spending, fewer orders, or changes in product mix.
